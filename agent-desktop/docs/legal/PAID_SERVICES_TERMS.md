# Cubecloud Paid Services Terms

Working draft for internal planning and counsel review. This document is not legal advice.

## Covered services

These terms are intended to govern optional Cubecloud-managed offerings layered on top of the local desktop product, including:

- managed agentic workflows;
- hosted skills packs and harnesses;
- managed MCP connectors and integrated services;
- hosted model routing, compatibility upkeep, and model maintenance;
- operational support, deployment assistance, and premium maintenance plans.

## Service model

1. The local desktop shell may remain free to use for basic self-hosted workflows.
2. Managed services, hosted integrations, premium connectors, and maintenance may require recurring fees, usage-based charges, enterprise contracts, or support retainers.
3. Access to paid services may be enforced through accounts, API keys, workspace entitlements, subscription checks, or organization-level contracts.

## Customer responsibilities

Customers are responsible for:

- providing lawful prompts, content, and connected data sources;
- obtaining rights to any third-party APIs, models, or content they connect;
- keeping local environments, credentials, and endpoint configuration secure;
- reviewing outputs before operational or commercial use.

## Service boundaries

Cubecloud paid services may include compatibility maintenance, connector updates, workflow templates, monitoring, and support. They do not guarantee correctness of model outputs, uninterrupted third-party APIs, or the legal suitability of customer prompts or generated content.

## Billing boundaries

Fees may attach to one or more of the following:

- workspace seats or operators;
- hosted connector usage;
- managed MCP endpoint usage;
- premium skills or workflow packs;
- model-routing or model-maintenance plans;
- support, onboarding, or integration services.

## Suspension and termination

Cubecloud may suspend or limit access to paid services for non-payment, abuse, security risk, excessive load, or violations of acceptable-use or contract terms. Suspension of paid services does not reduce any rights a customer independently holds under applicable open-source licenses for local code components.

## Change management

Cubecloud may modify paid services to maintain security, vendor compatibility, or product direction. Material commercial changes should be reflected in published plan documentation or customer contracts before enforcement.
